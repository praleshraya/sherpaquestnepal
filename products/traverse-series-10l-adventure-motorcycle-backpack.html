PRODUCT DETAILS

The Rhinowalk Traverse Series MCY2602 10L adventure motorcycle backpack combines compact organized storage, a stable X-Lock harness, hydration-tube routing and a removable cushioned back insert for off-road riding, touring and commuting. The matching MCY2603 chest pack is available on this page as a separate accessory option Hydration bladder is not included with the MCY2602 backpack. 10L organized riding storage Main compartment: approximately 6L. Waterproof roll-top upper pocket: approximately 3L. Independent front pocket: approximately 1L. External Hypalon attachment area for quick-access gear. Built for a stable riding fit X-Lock harness: adjustable shoulder straps, a magnetic chest buckle and detachable waist belt help control movement on uneven roads. Cushioned back insert: the removable ventilated insert adds support and impact cushioning. It should not be treated as a substitute for separately certified motorcycle body armor. Hydration-ready routing: secure a compatible hydration tube at the shoulder strap while using a separately purchased bladder. Hidden rain cover: pull the cover from the lower storage area when rain, dust or road spray increases. Chest-pack expansion: connect the separately purchased MCY2603 chest pack using the four designated straps. Emergency information card: record contact and medical information in the hidden shoulder-strap card.
