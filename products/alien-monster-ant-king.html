PRODUCT DETAILS

The ANT KING Gloves feature wear-resistant genuine leather and Yanwei microfiber for durability and comfort. The palm is equipped with silicone anti-slip for a secure grip, while conductive PU material enables touchscreen compatibility. Carbon fiber particles on the knuckles and an outer TPR protective shell with built-in carbon fiber protection provide enhanced impact resistance. XDR cushioning slow rebound and wrist cushion pad offer effective shock absorption. A thickened buffer block on the little finger adds extra protection. The breathable material ensures all-day comfort. Velcro design allows easy adjustment for a secure fit.
