PRODUCT DETAILS

The Rhinowalk expandable motorcycle travel tail bag series offers three rear-seat luggage sizes from 40ltr to 60ltr. Each black PVC bag expands through side zippers, uses a reinforced shape- supporting structure and includes mounting straps, a shoulder strap and a rain cover for touring and commuting. 40–60L – MT4060BK: 44 x 31.5 x 31.5 cm closed; 62 x 31.5 x 31.5 cm expanded; approximately 2,840 g. Designed for organized motorcycle travel Expandable storage: open the side expansion zippers when extra room is needed for longer trips. Wide access: a full zipper opening makes bulky equipment easier to load and reach. Independent side pockets: organize smaller or frequently used items away from the main compartment. Shape support: reinforced steel wire and PE panels help reduce sagging and movement. Stable mounting: four-point strap routing secures the bag to suitable rear-seat or rack anchor points. Wet-weather preparation: fit the included rain cover before prolonged or heavy rain. Off-bike carry: use the removable shoulder strap after taking the bag off the motorcycle.
