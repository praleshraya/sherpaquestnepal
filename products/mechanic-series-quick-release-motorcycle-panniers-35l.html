PRODUCT DETAILS

The Rhinowalk Mechanic Series quick-release motorcycle pannier system combines a semi-rigid 35L side bag with a slide-in mounting plate for fast removal from a compatible motorcycle rack. Choose one bag, a matched pair 35L pair, or the MP24003 rack extender accessory. Why choose the Mechanic Series pannier? Quick-release rack mounting: the reinforced back plate slides into its rack-mounted base so the bag can be removed without undoing soft straps. Two useful capacities: select 20L for a more compact setup or 35L for larger touring loads. Flexible pair options: buy one bag, two bags of the same size, or a mixed 20L/35L pair for motorcycles with different left- and right-side clearance. Waterproof inner storage: a seamless welded inner liner and roll-top closure protect packed gear when correctly closed. Semi-rigid structure: reinforced panels help the bag keep its shape while the front MOLLE area supports compatible external gear. Built for travel: a wide opening, carry handle, heavy-duty buckles and lock-compatible closures support touring and daily use. Rack compatibility and fit A compatible motorcycle side rack is required and is not included. The rack must provide enough flat mounting area, suitable fastener access and safe clearance from the exhaust, wheel, chain and suspension. The system is not universally compatible with every rack. Uneven, curved or asymmetrical racks may require the optional MP24003 extender. The right side of some BMW GS , cf moto 450 rack systems also requires an adapter. Materials and included equipment Outer construction: polyester with semi-rigid support. Inner storage: removable seamless welded waterproof liner. Bag options include the selected bag quantity, quick-release back plate, standard mounting hardware and combination lock(s) shown for that option. MP24003 is an adapter kit only and does not include a bag.
