ILM RL01 | ADVENTURE AIRFLOW ALL-SEASON ARMORED MOTORCYCLE TOURING JACKET CE-CERTIFIED PROTECTION • 600D OXFORD • MICRO MESH AIRFLOW • WATER-PROOF • WINDPROOF LINER • REFLECTIVE VISIBILITY Built for riders who refuse to let changing weather dictate the ride. The ILM RL01 Adventure Airflow combines abrasion-resistant 600D Oxford construction, high-airflow micro mesh, CE-certified impact protection and a removable windproof liner into one versatile riding jacket. Designed for adventure touring, long-distance rides, dirt-bike use and everyday motorcycle riding, the RL01 adapts as conditions change — from hot-weather airflow to cool and wet-weather protection. --- CE-CERTIFIED IMPACT PROTECTION PROTECTION WHERE IT MATTERS MOST. The RL01 is equipped with CE-certified internal armor at the key impact zones: - Shoulders — CE-certified protection - Elbows — CE-certified protection - Spine/Back — CE-certified protection The internal protectors are designed to help absorb and distribute impact energy while the jacket's abrasion-resistant outer construction provides an additional layer of protection. Protection without turning the jacket into a rigid suit. --- AIRFLOW ENGINEERED FOR HOT RIDING MESH WHERE YOU NEED THE AIR. Large micro mesh panels allow air to move through the jacket while riding, helping release accumulated heat during warm-weather and high-temperature rides. The ventilation system can be opened when maximum airflow is needed and closed when conditions become cooler. AIRFLOW • VENTILATION • CLIMATE ADAPTABILITY --- 600D OXFORD + MICRO MESH CONSTRUCTION BUILT FOR THE DEMANDS OF THE ROAD. The RL01 combines abrasion-resistant micro mesh with durable 600D Oxford fabric to balance airflow and protection. 600D Oxford reinforces the jacket's durable outer construction, while mesh panels maintain the airflow needed for summer touring and extended rides. DURABLE OUTER SHELL. HIGH-AIRFLOW RIDING ZONES. --- ALL-SEASON WEATHER ADAPTABILITY ONE JACKET. CHANGING CONDITIONS. The RL01 is designed around a versatile layering system. Its zip-in windproof liner provides additional protection against cold air and wet-weather conditions. Remove or open the liner when temperatures rise and increase airflow through the ventilation system. This allows the RL01 to transition between: HOT WEATHER → AIRFLOW MODE → COOL WEATHER → WET CONDITIONS The jacket is described as water-proof/weather-protective; . --- ADJUSTABLE RIDING FIT SECURE THE FIT. FOCUS ON THE RIDE. Adjustable straps allow the rider to fine-tune the jacket's fit over different layers and riding conditions. A more secure fit helps keep the protective armor positioned correctly while riding and allows the jacket to remain comfortable during long-distance use. --- 8 SECURE STORAGE POCKETS CARRY WHAT MATTERS. KEEP IT SECURE. The RL01 is designed with eight dedicated pockets: 5 INNER POCKETS + 3 OUTER ZIPPERED POCKETS The combination provides practical storage for everyday riding essentials while keeping frequently accessed items secure during touring and commuting. --- REFLECTIVE VISIBILITY DESIGNED TO BE SEEN AFTER DARK. Strategically placed reflective strips increase the jacket's visibility when illuminated by headlights and other road lighting. Especially valuable during: NIGHT RIDING • LOW-LIGHT CONDITIONS • RAIN • TOURING Visibility is another layer of riding awareness when sharing the road with other vehicles. --- BUILT FOR ADVENTURE. READY FOR EVERYDAY RIDING. From weekend exploration to long-distance touring, the RL01 is engineered as a versatile protective layer for riders who need airflow in the heat, protection on the road and adaptability when the weather changes.
