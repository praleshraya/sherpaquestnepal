PRODUCT DETAILS

The Rhinowalk MT21620 20L modular motorcycle tail bag is one compact rear-seat bag that converts to a shoulder bag or backpack. Its removable welded inner liner protects contents from rain, while external buckles allow it to connect with compatible bags from the same modular family. One bag, multiple uses 20L capacity in a 40 x 26 x 18 cm format. Four-point straps mount to a suitable passenger seat or rear rack. Two removable straps support shoulder or backpack carry. External mesh panel separates damp or frequently used items. Reflective branding improves low-light visibility. What is included One MT21620 bag, one waterproof inner liner, four buckle connectors, four fixing straps and two carry straps . The 2-in-1, 3-in-1 and 4-in-1 images demonstrate modular expansion; additional 10L, 20L or 30L bags are sold separately. Fit and safety Use a seat or rack that supports the bag footprint and provides stable anchor points. Keep every strap clear of the exhaust, wheel, chain and suspension, follow the vehicle or rack load limit and recheck tension during travel.
