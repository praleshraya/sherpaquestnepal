FEHER SJ2311 VENTILATED MESH RIDING JACKET MAXIMUM AIRFLOW. ESSENTIAL PROTECTION. BUILT FOR THE RIDE. Engineered for warm-weather motorcycle riding, the FEHER SJ2311 combines a durable 600D Oxford outer shell with high-flow mesh ventilation and removable CE-certified protection. Designed to keep you cool, protected and unrestricted from everyday commutes to long-distance touring. KEY FEATURES 600D OXFORD CONSTRUCTION Durable, abrasion-resistant outer fabric designed for everyday motorcycle use. HIGH-FLOW MESH VENTILATION Strategic diamond-mesh panels maximise airflow to keep you comfortable in warm riding conditions. CE-CERTIFIED PROTECTION Removable CE-certified protectors at the shoulders and elbows provide impact protection at key riding zones. ERGONOMIC RIDING FIT Stretch construction and a tailored profile allow natural movement without unnecessary restriction. REFLECTIVE DETAILS Integrated reflective elements enhance visibility in low-light riding conditions. ADJUSTABLE CUFFS Fine-tune the fit around the wrists and accommodate different glove configurations.
