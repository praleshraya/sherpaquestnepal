PRODUCT DETAILS

DOT & ECE Certified Motorcycle Helmet – Professional Safety Protection This motorcycle helmet meets DOT and ECE safety standards, delivering superior impact absorption, penetration resistance, and shock attenuation. The lightweight ABS shell combined with a high-density EPS liner ensures excellent strength while keeping the helmet comfortable for long rides. Perfect for street bikes, racing, motocross, ATVs, cruisers, and touring motorcycles. Dual Visor Helmet for All Light Conditions The advanced dual visor system features a clear outer shield for maximum visibility and an inner sun visor for UV and glare protection. The visor lock mechanism improves sealing performance, enhances riding safety, and reduces wind noise. A tinted replacement visor is included for versatile riding environments. Pinlock70 Anti-Fog System for Clear Vision This helmet includes a Pinlock70 anti-fog lens and mask, offering significantly better fog resistance than Pinlock30. It ensures clear vision in cold, rainy, and humid conditions, making it ideal for daily commuting and weekend touring. Advanced Ventilation for Maximum Comfort Air intakes at the mouth, front, top, and rear create a multi-channel airflow system that improves internal circulation, reduces heat buildup, and minimizes fogging. This ventilation design keeps riders cool and comfortable on long-distance rides.
