ABOUT SHERPA QUEST

BUILT AROUND
THE RIDE.

Sherpa Quest Nepal brings motorcycle accessories and riding gear together for individual riders, retailers and wholesale buyers. Our selection spans helmets, jackets, gloves, boots, rider tech, protection, touring luggage and the smaller essentials that make a journey easier.

From a daily commute to a long route through the mountains, we help people find equipment that fits their bike, their plans and their budget. Retail customers can ask about sizing and available colors; business buyers can discuss quantities and supply directly with our team.
